How it works

  1. You share your old resume, 1–3 job links you want, and answer a 10-question form (about 15 minutes)
  2. We ask follow-up questions to find your real numbers and achievements. We never invent them.
  3. Draft delivered, you review, we revise
  4. Final files + a 1-page "how to apply" checklist

Payment

50% advance and 50% before final delivery, both paid through the marketplace checkout (no UPI or direct transfers). Students: full payment through the marketplace checkout after the first draft is shown (watermarked).

Honest promises

  • We do not guarantee interviews or jobs. No one honestly can. We guarantee a clean, ATS-readable, keyword-matched resume and the agreed revisions.
  • We never add fake companies, degrees, skills or numbers. If you can't back something up in an interview, it doesn't go on the resume.
  • Not happy with the first draft and haven't approved it? We refund the advance in full.

Privacy

Your resume and phone number are used only to do this work. Files are deleted 30 days after delivery unless you ask us to keep them. Nothing is shared or sold.

Google Business Profile (GBP) setup guide for Indian small businesses

A plain-language, step-by-step guide. Written 15 Oct 2026 and checked against Google's official help pages; Google changes its steps often, so the official pages win if they differ. Prepared with AI assistance.

Why this matters: when someone searches "clinic near me" or "salon in Koramangala", Google shows a Maps listing before the websites. A free, complete profile is often what makes the phone ring. It costs ₹0. Anyone who tells you Google charges a fee for a listing is wrong.

Official sources: - Add or claim your profile: https://support.google.com/business/answer/2911778 - Verification: https://support.google.com/business/answer/7107242 - Guidelines (what's allowed): https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177

Before you start (10 minutes)

  • [ ] A Google Account (Gmail or any email address) that the owner controls. Don't use the agency's or nephew's account.
  • [ ] The exact business name as written on your signboard. Don't add keywords like "Best Clinic Noida", which breaks Google's rules and can get the listing suspended.
  • [ ] Full address with pincode, and a phone number that is answered during business hours
  • [ ] Opening hours, including Sunday and lunch breaks
  • [ ] 5–10 real photos: signboard/outside (helps people find you), inside, team, products/dishes
  • [ ] Your website link (for example the demo site) and WhatsApp number

Step 1: Create or claim

  1. Go to google.com/business, then Manage now, and sign in.
  2. Type your business name. If it already appears (Google sometimes auto-creates one), choose it and claim it. Otherwise choose Add your business.
  3. Pick the primary category carefully: it matters most. Examples: Medical clinic, Beauty salon, Maharashtrian restaurant / Tiffin center. You can add up to 9 extra categories later.

Step 2: Location

  • Customers visit you (clinic, salon, restaurant): add the address and drag the pin onto your exact shop.
  • You go to customers (home tiffin, mobile bridal makeup): choose service area and hide your home address.

Step 3: Verify

Google picks the method; you can't always choose. Common ones in India today: - Live video call with Google support, where offered: you show the shop and proof of management live. - Video recording: one continuous clip showing the signboard outside, the street, the inside of the shop, and proof you manage it (e.g. unlocking the shutter, the billing counter, tools of the trade). Keep it under ~2 minutes, unedited. - Phone/SMS or email code (less common now) - Postcard (slow; can take 2–3 weeks)

Review can take a few days. Don't create a second profile while you wait, because duplicates cause suspensions.

Step 4: Complete everything (this is what ranks)

Field Tip
Description (750 chars) Say who you help and how, in plain words. Mention the area naturally.
Hours + special hours Update for Diwali, Eid, Christmas and other holiday closures. Wrong hours = angry customers.
Phone, website, WhatsApp Use "Book" / "Order" links to the website form if offered
Products / Services / Menu Add prices where possible. People in India love seeing the price upfront.
Photos Add 1–2 fresh photos every week. Real photos beat stock images.
Attributes Wheelchair access, women-owned, UPI accepted, etc.

Step 5: Reviews: the honest way

  • Ask happy customers in person: "If you liked it, a Google review really helps us." Share your review link (GBP dashboard, then Ask for reviews) on WhatsApp or as a QR code at the counter.
  • Reply to every review, including bad ones, calmly and within 2 days.
  • Never buy reviews, write your own, or give discounts in exchange for reviews. It's against Google's policy, and fake reviews can lead to removal or a public warning on your profile.
  • Clinics: don't share any patient medical details in replies.

Step 6: Keep it alive (15 minutes a week)

  • Post an update or offer ("Free BP check this Saturday", "Diwali sweets thali")
  • The public Q&A section has been phased out (Google announced it in Dec 2025, and the Q&A API was discontinued on 3 Nov 2025: https://developers.google.com/my-business/content/sunset-dates). Customers now use Google's AI Ask feature, which answers from your profile, website and reviews, so keep your description, services, hours and website FAQs complete and current. If your dashboard still shows a Q&A section with existing questions, you can answer them there.
  • Check Performance to see calls, direction requests and searches

Common mistakes

  1. Keyword-stuffed names, which get the listing suspended
  2. Using a mobile number nobody picks up
  3. Pin in the wrong lane, so customers get lost
  4. Letting an agency own the account. Always add them as a Manager, never transfer Primary owner.
  5. Ignoring reviews
